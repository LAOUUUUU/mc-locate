# Mode 14 — Documentation

You are reading it.

Every mode's full write-up is embedded in the binary, so it works offline and
without the repository. Each page covers:

* **What the mode does** and when you would reach for it.
* **What it needs**, and where that comes from in game — which F3 line, which
  key, what to look at.
* **How it works**, including the actual formulas and why they are what they
  are.
* **Its limits**, stated plainly. Several modes are estimates rather than
  answers, and those say so.
* **What it feeds**, since the modes are designed to chain.

There are also two pages that are not about a single mode:

* **Overview** — how the modes fit together, and the two or three routes people
  actually take through them.
* **Glossary** — structure seed versus world seed, salts, regions, bit-lifting,
  pillar seeds, and the rest of the vocabulary the other pages assume.

If you are new: read the overview, then try mode 11, which needs nothing and
finishes instantly.
